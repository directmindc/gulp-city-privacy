Gulp City: Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

The short version

  • Gulp City is a game for all ages, children included.
  • The game never asks for your name, email address, photos, contacts or location, and there are no accounts.
  • Your progress, scores and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as coming from a child, so the ads are never personalised and are limited to ads suitable for everyone.
  • There are no in-app purchases, no chat and no social features.

Who we are

Gulp City ("the game") is made by Poison Gaming ("we", "us"), a sole proprietorship in Durban, South Africa. This policy covers the Gulp City app for Android. If you have a question about it, write to directmindc@gmail.com.

Our Information Officer under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is Daniel Russell, registered with the Information Regulator under number 2026-069741, and can be reached at the same address.

What the game itself collects

Nothing that identifies you. The game does not ask for or read any personal information, and we do not run any servers that receive data from it.

The game saves a few things on your device so you can carry on where you left off:

  • which regions you have cleared, with your best times and scores
  • your settings, such as sound volume and how your hole looks

This stays in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Ads

The game is free because it shows ads. They come from Google AdMob, an advertising service run by Google. There are two kinds: a full-screen ad after each region you clear, and optional video ads you can choose to watch to start the next region giant, open a region early or unlock a new look for your hole. Ads need an internet connection. Without one, the game still plays and simply shows no ads.

Because Gulp City is for all ages, the game treats every player as a child when it asks Google for an ad:

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent.
  • Ads are not personalised. They are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps or websites.
  • Only ads rated as suitable for general audiences are requested.
  • The game does not use your device's advertising ID. It removes the permission that would allow it.

Where the law asks for it, for example in Europe and the United Kingdom, the game uses Google's consent tools before it asks for ads. If they give you choices, a "Privacy choices" button in the game's Settings lets you change them at any time.

To deliver ads at all, Google's advertising software inside the game automatically processes some technical information:

InformationWhy Google processes it
Internet (IP) address, which also shows roughly where a device is, such as the country or cityTo send the ad to the device, show ads allowed in that country, and detect fraud
Device and app details, such as phone model, Android version, language, screen size and game versionTo pick an ad that fits the device and displays correctly
Ad activity, such as which ads were shown and whether one was tapped or watchedTo avoid repeating the same ad too often, and to count views for advertisers
Diagnostics, such as loading times and errors in the ad softwareTo keep ads working and fix problems
Identifiers that are not the advertising ID, such as an ID tied to this app's installationTo limit how often an ad appears and to prevent fraud and abuse

This information goes directly from the device to Google. We do not receive it, and we cannot use it to identify a player. Google handles it under its own rules:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Tapping an ad can open the Google Play store or a website outside the game. Those places have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Children should ask a parent or guardian before tapping an ad.

Children's privacy

Gulp City is suitable for all ages and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else. The game has no accounts, no chat, no way to share or post anything, and no purchases.

The ads follow the child-directed settings described above for every player, whatever their age, in line with children's privacy laws such as the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe and the United Kingdom, and POPIA in South Africa.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question or concern about your child's privacy in the game, write to directmindc@gmail.com.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • It does not use the camera or microphone.
  • It does not include analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • It does not sell personal information.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibration: for a short buzz when your hole grows.
  • Keeping the device awake and running in the foreground: used by Google's ad software while it loads or shows an ad.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your saved progress and settings live only on your device. To erase them, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the game.

Google keeps the technical information described under "Ads" for as long as its own policies say. The links above explain how to contact Google and what choices it offers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, the law may give you rights over your personal information, such as asking to see it, correct it or have it deleted. Because we do not hold any personal information about players, we usually cannot identify you in order to act on such a request, but write to us and we will help where we can, including pointing you to the right place at Google.

If you are in South Africa and are not happy with how we have handled a privacy question, you can complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za.

Security

The game sends no personal information to us. Information that Google's ad software sends to Google is encrypted in transit.

Where information is processed

Google may process the information described under "Ads" on servers in countries other than your own. Google's privacy policy explains how it protects information when it does this.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, for example if it adds a new feature, we will update this page and change the date at the top before the change reaches players.
